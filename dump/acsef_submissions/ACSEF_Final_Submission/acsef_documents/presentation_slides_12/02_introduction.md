## Text to display
- Research question: Can one biologically informed graph architecture outperform strict baselines across multiple disease cohorts while remaining interpretable?
- What is known: Transcriptomic classifiers often lose accuracy when moved to new cohorts because of platform and batch domain shift.
- Hypothesis: Multiplex biological priors (KEGG, STRING, co-expression) plus domain-aware training improve cross-cohort robustness.
- Project scope: Primary development in sepsis, then transfer evaluation in Alzheimer's disease and osteogenesis imperfecta.
